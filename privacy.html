Privacy policy

Sparkbot Inbox uses Google OAuth to access the mailbox its owner authorizes. It reads messages and related metadata, such as sender, subject, and date, to prepare inbox summaries and identify messages that may need the owner's attention.

The owner's Sparkbot host stores OAuth credentials to keep the connection available between scheduled runs. The inbox monitor stores processing checkpoints and message identifiers to avoid reporting the same mail twice. When Sparkbot posts a brief to Discord, the selected details and summaries in the brief are shared with Discord and are visible to people with access to that destination.

Sparkbot Inbox uses Gmail data for these inbox features. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can disconnect Sparkbot Inbox from Google Account connections and delete locally stored credentials, checkpoints, and run records from the host.
